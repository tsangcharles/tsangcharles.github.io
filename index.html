Charles Tsang

Charles is a full-stack data generalist, equally comfortable wrangling pipelines, building models, and shipping AI-powered products. He also teaches as a Data Science Lecturer for the Master of Mathematical Finance program at the University of Toronto. Charles has previously worked at Instagram (Meta), Zynga, EY, and KPMG.

Besides work, Charles is a big fan of Pokemon and Digimon. In his free time, he also enjoys playing basketball and soccer.

Charles has a Golden Doodle named Pudding, he is very energetic and rarely listens to commands.
