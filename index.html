Charles Tsang

Charles is a Staff Data Scientist at Glass Lewis, where he builds AI tools that turn messy documents into structured data. Before that, he did product analytics at Instagram and Zynga, and consulting at EY and KPMG. He also teaches data science in the Master of Mathematical Finance program at the University of Toronto.

Besides work, Charles is a big fan of Pokemon and Digimon. In his free time, he also enjoys playing basketball and soccer.

Charles has a Golden Doodle named Pudding, he is very energetic and rarely listens to commands.
