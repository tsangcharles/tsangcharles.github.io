How I teach

I start with examples. Always. A definition with nothing to refer to is just notation — the example is what gives it somewhere to live.

1

Feel the need first

Before introducing a concept, I put students in a position where they need it. Once the question is alive in the room, the definition stops feeling arbitrary and starts feeling inevitable.

2

Connect it to practice

Industry work in ads, fraud detection and customer analytics showed me what actually matters. I bring that into class so students have a real reason to care.

3

Breadth, on purpose

Depth is easy to pursue alone, especially with AI tools. What's hard to get alone is the map: what exists, how it connects, and where each piece gets used.
